## 6. Salesforce AI Research: Random Attention Boosts vLLM Throughput 32–43%

**Category:** Research
**Source:** [arXiv listing — cs.AI September 2026](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/current) · [Kaggle ArXiv Dataset 2025–2026](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/shree0910/arxiv-aiml-research-papers-20252026)

Salesforce AI Research published a paper introducing Random Attention, a KV cache eviction policy that preserves only the initial system prompt tokens and evicts all other tokens per attention head by uniform random sampling — requiring no scoring pass. In vLLM deployments, this raises serving throughput by 32–43% while maintaining reasoning accuracy on par with or better than signal-based eviction strategies.

**Why it matters:** KV cache eviction is a critical bottleneck in long-context LLM serving. The result that random eviction matches or beats complex learned or heuristic policies is both surprising and practically useful — it eliminates a scoring overhead that adds latency and memory pressure at scale.

## 7. Manus Resumes Independent Operations After China Forces Meta to Unwind $2B Deal

**Category:** Other
**Source:** [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/11/manus-china-meta-acquisition.html) · [O'Melveny Legal Analysis](https://www.omm.com/insights/alerts-publications/china-unwinds-meta-s-acquisition-of-manus-implications-for-cross-border-ai-transactions/) · [TradingKey](https://www.tradingkey.com/analysis/stocks/us-stocks/262096576-manus-meta-acquisition-unwound-china-regulator-tradingkey)

In early September, Manus — the Singapore-incorporated AI agent company originally founded in China — formally resumed independent operations after China's NDRC in April ordered Meta to unwind its December 2025 $2 billion acquisition. The unwinding process included mandatory deletion of user data generated during Meta's ownership period.

**Why it matters:** This is the first major case of China exercising foreign-investment review authority to claw back an AI acquisition by a US firm, with direct implications for cross-border M&A involving AI companies with Chinese origins. It signals that the geopolitical layer of AI governance now extends to corporate structure and capital flows, not just export controls.

## 9. HyperWorld: Hypergraph State Serialization Improves Textual World Models

**Category:** Research
**Source:** [arXiv — cs.AI September 2026](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/current)

A paper titled "HyperWorld: Hypergraph-Structured State Serialization Improves Learned Textual World Models" appeared on arXiv in early September 2026, proposing a novel state representation using hypergraphs to encode structured relationships in environments used by language-model-based agents.

**Why it matters:** World models are a key bottleneck for agentic systems that must plan, reason about long-horizon actions, and maintain consistent environmental state across steps. Hypergraph-structured representations could offer more expressive and compact state serialization than flat text, improving planning fidelity in agent loops.
