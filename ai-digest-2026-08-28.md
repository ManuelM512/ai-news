## Research

### 7. DeepMind Publishes Method to Reduce "Reasoning Collapse" by ~40%

**Source:** [AI Research Highlights — Skycrumbs Blog](https://skycrumbs.com/blog/ai-research-august-2026)

A DeepMind team published research on "reasoning collapse" — the failure mode in which LLMs give different answers to logically equivalent problems phrased differently — and proposes a training methodology that reduces this brittleness by approximately 40% on their evaluation set.

**Why it matters:** Inconsistent reasoning on semantically equivalent queries is a reliability blocker for deployed reasoning agents; a robust mitigation would directly improve trustworthiness in production systems.

### 8. arXiv: Certifying Collective Reasoning in Multi-Agent Systems via Koopman Spectral Analysis

**Source:** [arXiv:2608.05956](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.MA/current)

A new paper proposes using Koopman operator spectral analysis to formally certify that a group of cooperating agents will collectively reach correct conclusions — providing provable guarantees rather than empirical testing for multi-agent reasoning chains.

**Why it matters:** As multi-agent pipelines handle higher-stakes decisions, formal verification methods that can certify emergent group behavior become increasingly important for safe deployment.
