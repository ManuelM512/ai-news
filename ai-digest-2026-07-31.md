## New Framework

### 1. MCP Spec 2026-07-28 Officially Ships — Largest Protocol Revision Since Launch
**Source:** [Model Context Protocol Blog](https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2026-07-28/) · [Cloudflare Changelog](https://developers.cloudflare.com/changelog/post/2026-07-27-agents-sdk-v0.20.0-mcp-sdk-v2/) · [WorkOS Blog](https://workos.com/blog/mcp-2026-spec-agent-authentication) · [Stacktree](https://stacktr.ee/blog/mcp-2026-spec-changes)

The final MCP 2026-07-28 specification shipped on July 28, the largest revision to the protocol since its launch. The initialize/initialized handshake is removed entirely — client capabilities now travel in `_meta` on every request — and the `Mcp-Session-Id` header is gone, making the protocol stateless by default. A new `server/discover` method lets clients fetch server capabilities on demand. The spec also introduces MCP Apps (server-rendered UIs), a Tasks extension for long-running agentic workflows, and authorization more tightly aligned with OAuth and OIDC. Beta SDKs in Python, TypeScript, Go, and C# are now available for the new spec.

**Why it matters:** Stateless MCP dramatically simplifies server deployment — no sticky sessions or Durable Objects required — and the Tasks extension formally makes long-running autonomous workflows a first-class protocol feature. This will reshape AI agent infrastructure for the second half of 2026.

Stairwell launched Backstory on July 29, an agentic investigation platform built for malware response. The tool automates the lateral analysis of related malware variants, designed around research showing 2.4 related variants exist for every sample named in public threat reports — meaning the vast majority of related threats never surface in manual investigation.

**Why it matters:** Automated traversal of the variant graph is a meaningful force multiplier for security teams overwhelmed by triage. Surfaces threats that would otherwise remain invisible until they re-emerge.

## Research

### 5. ICML 2026: Selective Activation Sparsity Lets Small Models Match Models 3× Their Size
**Source:** [Skycrumbs Blog](https://skycrumbs.com/blog/ai-research-july-2026) · ICML 2026

A top-cited result from ICML 2026 this month is a training method called "selective activation sparsity" that teaches models to activate only the subset of parameters most relevant to each specific task. On reasoning benchmarks, models trained with this method performed comparably to models three times their size.

**Why it matters:** If results generalize across task types and model scales, selective activation sparsity could substantially change the cost curve for AI deployment — especially in latency-sensitive or on-device applications where running large models is impractical.
