## New Model

### 1. Meta Releases Muse Spark Open Weights
**Source:** [SiliconAngle](https://siliconangle.com/?p=850737) · [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2026/09/03/meta-launches-muse-spark-1-3-with-faster-coding-and-smarter-agents/)

Meta released open weights for Muse Spark, its flagship coding and long-running agent model, on October 15. The model is a 816B-parameter sparse MoE with 42B active per token, supports a 1M-token context window, and was trained with safety post-training so it operates without Meta's internal guardrails. Meta claims it is the most capable open-weight model released by a US lab to date.

**Why it matters:** A frontier-scale open-weight model optimised for coding agents entering the open ecosystem is a significant shift — it gives the community a strong base for fine-tuning and self-hosted agent infrastructure.

### 2. StepFun Releases Step 5 Preview Open Weights
**Source:** [DevelopersDigest](https://www.developersdigest.tech/blog/stepfun-step-5-preview-open-weights-oct-15-2026) · [OrcaRouter](https://www.orcarouter.ai/pl/blog/step-5-preview-open-weights)

StepFun released BF16 open weights for Step 5 Preview on October 15. The model is a 600B-parameter sparse MoE (27B active per token) with a 1M-token context window, priced at $1.00/$2.70 per million input/output tokens via API. Its license is reportedly a `stepfun-community-license` — users should verify terms before commercial deployment.

**Why it matters:** Two massive open-weight frontier models from Meta and StepFun landing the same day accelerates access to top-tier models outside the closed API paradigm.

### 3. DeepSeek Releases V4.1-Flash-1017 Checkpoint and Opens Search/Fetch APIs
**Source:** [Rio Rundown Substack — Oct 18](https://riorundown.substack.com/p/ai-news-oct-18-2026-open-models-and)

A new DeepSeek V4.1-Flash checkpoint (tagged 1017) was released today alongside DeepSeek opening its Search and Fetch APIs to all developers. DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash is a ~552B sparse MoE with native multimodal input, a 1M-token context, and MIT-licensed weights.

**Why it matters:** Broader API access for DeepSeek's search and web-fetch capabilities lowers the barrier for building grounded, tool-using agents on top of one of the most cost-efficient frontier models available.

## New Agent Tool

### 4. Microsoft Agent Framework Python 1.22.0
**Source:** [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/microsoft/agent-framework/releases) · [AI Agent Frameworks — Fall 2026 Roundup](https://dreaming.press/posts/ai-agent-frameworks-whats-changed-fall-2026.html)

Microsoft shipped Agent Framework Python 1.22.0 on October 15, adding a new Cosmos DB memory provider, configurable service URLs for chat clients, and improvements to multi-agent workflow orchestration. The .NET counterpart reached 1.25.0 on October 14.

**Why it matters:** The addition of a managed Cosmos DB memory backend makes it significantly easier to give agents durable, scalable memory without standing up custom infrastructure.

## Research

### 5. OpenAI Shares AI Progress in Mathematics: 722 Manuscripts with Lean Proofs
**Source:** [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/sharing-ai-progress-in-mathematics/) · [DataCamp](https://www.datacamp.com/blog/openai-math-breakthroughs-what-the-latest-results-mean) · [SesameDisk](https://sesamedisk.com/ai-breakthroughs-in-math-progress/)

OpenAI released 722 mathematics manuscripts produced by an internal frontier model, covering results across 372 research families. Many proofs are formally verified in Lean; unformalized results carry explicit uncertainty warnings. The release reportedly includes a claimed proof of the Unique Games Conjecture (unverified by the community at time of writing).

**Why it matters:** Mass-scale AI-assisted formal mathematics, with machine-checkable proofs, marks a meaningful step toward AI as a genuine research collaborator rather than a tool — though community verification of the most ambitious claims is ongoing.

### 6. arXiv: WorkflowPerturb — Stress Tests for Multi-Agent Workflow Metrics
**Source:** [Microsoft Research / EMNLP Industry Track](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/research-area/artificial-intelligence/)

Microsoft and CMU researchers submitted WorkflowPerturb to EMNLP's Industry Track. The paper describes a suite of perturbation tests that expose blind spots in standard multi-agent workflow evaluation metrics, showing many metrics are not robust to input rephrasing or execution order changes.

**Why it matters:** As multi-agent pipelines become production infrastructure, understanding where their evaluation metrics fail is critical for building reliable systems.

## Other

### 7. Character.AI Files Chapter 11; Disney Is Stalking-Horse Bidder at $20M
**Source:** [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/characterai-files-chapter-11-after-27b-google-deal) · [BetaNews](https://betanews.com/article/character-ai-bankruptcy-chapter-11/) · [OfficeChai](https://officechai.com/ai/character-ai-founded-by-transformers-paper-author-noam-shazeer-files-for-chapter-11-bankruptcy/)

Character Technologies Inc. filed for Chapter 11 on October 12 in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for Delaware, estimating assets and liabilities at $50–100M. Disney is the stalking-horse bidder at $20M for selected IP. The platform will continue operating during proceedings; a sale is expected to close in late November or early December. The filing follows Google's 2024 licensing deal that brought co-founders Noam Shazeer and Daniel de Freitas to Google for ~$2.7B.

**Why it matters:** The collapse of one of AI's highest-profile consumer chat startups underlines the gap between peak valuations and sustainable business models for open-ended conversational AI products.
