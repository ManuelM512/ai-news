## New Model

### 1. MiniMax M3.1 Flash Launches with 1M-Token Context
**Source:** [DataNorth](https://datanorth.ai/news/minimax-releases-m3-1-flash-preview) · [LLM Gateway](https://llmgateway.io/models/minimax-m3.1-flash)

MiniMax released M3.1 Flash on October 19, a multimodal model designed for agentic reasoning, tool use, coding, and long-context tasks. It supports up to 1,000,000 tokens of context, with tunable thinking depth and API pricing of $0.15 / $0.60 per million input/output tokens. Open weights are scheduled for October 23, 2026.

**Why it matters:** A 1M-context model with competitive pricing strengthens the case for long-horizon agent workflows, and the pending open-weight release will let developers self-host without vendor lock-in.

### 2. Nvidia Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B — 74 Billion Parameters in 24 GB
**Source:** [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/?p=22840568) · [pasqualepillitteri.it](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/24182/nemotron-3-5-bolt-74b-nvidia-rtx-spark-launch)

Nvidia launched Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B-A9B on October 15, timed to coincide with RTX Spark laptop availability. The mixture-of-experts model has 74B total parameters but only ~9B active per token, compressed to 2-bit quantization to fit in 24 GB of VRAM. It targets local AI agent workloads and is compatible with llama.cpp, Ollama, LM Studio, and vLLM. Nvidia reports it scores 51.0 on Terminal-Bench 2.1, ahead of Qwen3.6-35B-A3B (44.4) and Gemma4 26B-A4B (37.2).

**Why it matters:** Running a 74B-class model on a laptop GPU lowers the barrier for on-device agents significantly, and the Terminal-Bench lead suggests meaningful real-world coding performance.

## New Framework

### 3. DeepSeek Open-Sources DeepInfer Inference Engine
**Source:** [Dig.Watch](https://dig.watch/updates/ai-firm-deepseek-opens-up-on-model-deployment-tech) · [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today/edition/2026-10-20)

DeepSeek released DeepInfer, described as the high-performance inference framework that powers its large-scale production services, under the Apache 2.0 license on GitHub. It supports Nvidia SM100 GPUs and Huawei Ascend 950 NPUs, and the release follows an earlier commitment to contribute design improvements and select components as standalone libraries.

**Why it matters:** Opening a battle-tested production inference stack—one that reportedly drives one of the world's highest-traffic LLM services—gives the community a reference implementation for extreme-throughput serving.

### 4. Google Ships TorchTPU to Court PyTorch Developers
**Source:** [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today/edition/2026-10-20) · [AI Agent Store](https://aiagentstore.ai/ai-agent-news/today)

Google has begun shipping TorchTPU, a library that lets PyTorch code run natively on TPU hardware with minimal changes. The effort targets AI developers currently locked into Nvidia workflows who want to explore Google's TPU infrastructure without rewriting training or inference pipelines.

**Why it matters:** If TorchTPU lowers the migration cost, it could meaningfully shift training workloads to TPUs, increasing competitive pressure on Nvidia's GPU dominance in the cloud AI market.

## New Agent Tool

### 5. Notion Agents Now Run for 24+ Hours with Model Routing
**Source:** [AI Agent Store — October 20, 2026](https://aiagentstore.ai/ai-agent-news/today)

Notion's latest agent update extends maximum task duration from 5 minutes to over 24 hours, enabling complex multi-step workflows that span document retrieval, editing, and external tool calls. The update also adds a model router that selects the cheapest capable model for each subtask, plus a two-tier credit system and bring-your-own-key (BYOK) support for enterprise customers.

**Why it matters:** Long-running agent tasks are a persistent gap in productivity tools; removing the 5-minute ceiling—and pairing it with cost-aware routing—makes Notion a credible alternative to custom agent pipelines for knowledge workers.

## Research

### 7. Claude Runs 64-Hour Autonomous Protein Design Campaign
**Source:** [AI Weekly — Oct 21](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today) · [AI Weekly — Oct 20](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today/edition/2026-10-20)

Anthropic reports that Claude autonomously ran a 64-hour protein design campaign, orchestrating 12 tools to generate 318,484 candidate protein sequences. Of 211 designs sent to the lab for testing, 17 successfully bound to neurotoxins from multiple scorpion species—demonstrating end-to-end autonomous scientific experimentation at scale.

**Why it matters:** Closing the loop between AI-driven design and wet-lab validation, entirely without human steering, is a landmark result for autonomous scientific agents and points toward AI-accelerated drug and antivenom discovery.

## Other

### 8. Anthropic-Backed AI Safety Bill Would Block High-Casualty Models
**Source:** [TechCrunch AI](https://techcrunch.com/category/artificial-intelligence/)

A U.S. legislative proposal backed by Anthropic would give regulators authority to block or pause deployment of AI models if credible evidence suggests they could directly enable harm to 50 or more people. The bill is still in early stages, with details under negotiation.

**Why it matters:** This is one of the first concrete legislative attempts to tie model deployment to quantified casualty thresholds, and Anthropic's backing signals that at least one frontier lab is willing to accept binding safety oversight.
