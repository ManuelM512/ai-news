### 8. T2SPO: Trajectory-to-Step Policy Optimisation for Agentic RL
**Source:** [arXiv:2610.00388](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00388)

T2SPO proposes a reinforcement learning method that decomposes long agent trajectories into step-level credit assignments, addressing the sparse-reward problem in long-horizon agent tasks.

**Why it matters:** Credit assignment over long trajectories is one of the central bottlenecks in training useful agents; practical progress here could significantly improve RL-trained agent quality.
