### 8. arXiv Caps Submissions at Two Papers Per Month to Fight AI Slop
**Source:** [404 Media](https://404media.co/arxiv-is-rate-limiting-submissions-because-it-cant-keep-up-with-ai-slop/) · [Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/ai-news/arxiv-limits-researchers-to-two-papers-a-month/) · [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/arxiv-caps-authors-at-two-papers-a-month-to-curb-ai-slop)

Effective October 1, arXiv limits each submitter to two papers per calendar month and three active submissions at once, across all categories. September 2026 saw 40,363 submissions — double the year-ago figure — overwhelming volunteer moderators who handled nearly 9,000 support tickets in one month alone.

**Why it matters:** The cap is a structural response to the AI-generated paper flood and sets a precedent for how academic infrastructure will manage AI-assisted research volume; it will affect legitimate prolific researchers alongside bad actors, sparking debate about the right moderation model.
