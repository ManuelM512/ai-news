## Research

### 6. Google Research Releases PaperOrchestra: Multi-Agent Paper Writing Framework
**Source:** [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.05018) · [Google Research](https://research.google/pubs/paperorchestra-a-multi-agent-framework-for-automated-paper-writing/) · [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/363837/googles-paperorchestra-ai-converts-lab-notes-into-publication-ready-research-papers)

Google Research published PaperOrchestra, a multi-agent framework that transforms unstructured lab notes and experiment logs into publication-ready LaTeX manuscripts. Five specialized agents handle outline planning, plotting, literature review (grounded via Semantic Scholar), section writing, and content refinement. Google claims 90–99% win rates over autonomous baselines on literature review depth and 14–38% improvement on overall manuscript quality by human evaluation, though Decrypt reports more modest gains on literature review margins (50–68%).

**Why it matters:** Automating the most labor-intensive parts of academic writing could dramatically accelerate research iteration cycles. Released under Apache 2.0, it represents a concrete step toward AI as a first-class research collaborator rather than just a writing aid.
