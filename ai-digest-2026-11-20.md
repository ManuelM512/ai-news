# AI News Digest — 2026-11-20

## New Model

### 1. Google Launches Gemini 4 Neon — Mid-Tier Model for Coding and Agent Work
**Source:** [Google Blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-neon/) · [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/18/gemini-4-neon-announcement/)

Google released Gemini 4 Neon on November 18, the mid-tier model in the Gemini 4 family, sitting between the high-end Carbon (for hard long-horizon projects) and lightweight Hydrogen (for fast everyday work). It is available via the Gemini API, AI Studio, Antigravity, and Android Studio at $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens — Google claims it costs 32% less per task than Gemini 4 Carbon at high thinking.

**Why it matters:** Neon fills an important cost-performance gap in the Gemini 4 lineup and can now serve as a planner in Google Antigravity's orchestrator paired with Hydrogen as the executor, making multi-model agent pipelines cheaper to run.

### 2. OpenAI Ships GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol
**Source:** [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-sol/) · [DigitalTrends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/computing/openai-gpt-6-2-astra-sol-launch/) · [WCCFTech](https://wccftech.com/openais-gpt-6-2-astra-becomes-the-first-model-to-achieve-a-100-score-on-arc-agi-3-while-being-8x-cheaper-than-anthropics-fable-5-2-but-actual-users-are-not-impressed/)

OpenAI released GPT-6.2 on November 17 as two models: Astra (frontier, for complex professional work) and Sol (everyday use). Astra is reportedly the first model to score 100 on ARC-AGI-3 and is priced 20% below its GPT-6 Astra predecessor ($8/$40 per million input/output tokens). The release came about seven weeks after OpenAI cancelled GPT-6.1 Astra before it shipped. Both models meet OpenAI's Critical cybersecurity threshold and are monitored through an internal system called Control Room.

**Why it matters:** GPT-6.2 Astra is OpenAI's latest frontier push with notable benchmark gains, though early user reception has been mixed — some report needing other models to redo Astra's subagent work, suggesting real-world agentic reliability still needs improvement.

**Source:** [LLM Gateway Timeline](https://llmgateway.io/timeline)

Alibaba released Qwen4-Max on approximately November 16, its most capable model in the Qwen4 series. The model is positioned as a frontier-class offering targeting competitive performance across reasoning and coding benchmarks.

## New Agent Tool

### 4. Microsoft Perception Security Platform Goes GA at Ignite 2026
**Source:** [Microsoft](https://news.microsoft.com/source/2026/11/10/ibm-and-microsoft-announce-a-new-ai-agent-platform-designed-for-industry-specific-transformation/) · [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/microsoft-takes-perception-agentic-security-platform-out-of-preview/)

Microsoft made its Perception agentic security platform generally available at Ignite 2026 (around November 18). Perception coordinates red, blue, and green team AI agents for security operations — its Alert Triage Agent reportedly handles about 80% of alerts in a typical Microsoft Defender queue, with the Web App Pen Test Agent now in preview.

**Why it matters:** Perception represents a push toward fully agentic security operations centers (SOCs), where AI agents handle the bulk of triage and testing workloads, dramatically reducing analyst toil for high-volume alert environments.

### 5. OpenAI Winds Down AgentKit — Agent Builder and Evals Retiring November 30
**Source:** [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-agentkit/)

OpenAI announced it is winding down its Agent Builder and Evals products from AgentKit, with both unavailable on the OpenAI platform starting November 30, 2026. Users are directed to the Agents SDK for code-based workflows and to Workspace Agents in ChatGPT for natural-language use cases.

**Why it matters:** This marks the end of AgentKit's no-code agent builder less than 14 months after launch, signaling that OpenAI is consolidating its agent tooling around the Agents SDK and native ChatGPT experiences rather than a standalone builder product.

## Research

### 6. arXiv: "Ambient LLMs" — Data-Efficient Fine-Tuning via Temperature Matching (2611.23229)
**Source:** [arXiv Digest 2026-11-20](https://github.com/HuggerHustle/ai-radar/issues/282)

This paper proposes Ambient LLMs, a data-efficient fine-tuning method using temperature matching, framed as a solution for post-training as pre-training data growth hits a plateau.

**Why it matters:** As compute and data scaling curves flatten, more efficient fine-tuning methods are increasingly critical. If the temperature-matching approach generalizes, it could reduce the data requirements for domain adaptation of large models.

---

### 7. arXiv: "A CPU Inside an LLM" — Zero-Gradient Circuit Grafting (Sah et al.)
**Source:** [arXiv Digest 2026-11-20](https://github.com/datnguyenquy94/news-radar/issues/1145)

The paper introduces zero-gradient circuit-grafting primitives that compile deterministic computation into frozen LLM weights, targeting exact multi-digit arithmetic without gradient fine-tuning.

**Why it matters:** Embedding verifiable deterministic logic into frozen model weights — rather than relying on prompting or fine-tuning — could meaningfully improve reliability for arithmetic-heavy agentic tasks without changing model weights during inference.

### 8. arXiv: "Mapping the AI Supply Chain" (2611.24927)
**Source:** [arXiv Digest 2026-11-20](https://github.com/datnguyenquy94/news-radar/issues/1145)

This paper documents actor and component relationships across the AI stack, revealing concentration and vulnerability across the supply chain from hardware to frontier models.

**Why it matters:** As AI governance and policy discussions accelerate globally, empirical supply chain maps are essential for policymakers and practitioners to identify chokepoints and systemic risks.

### 9. IBM and Microsoft Launch Imagine Enterprise — Agentic Platform for Industry
**Source:** [IBM Newsroom](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-11-10-ibm-and-microsoft-announce-a-new-ai-agent-platform-designed-for-industry-specific-transformation/) · [Microsoft](https://news.microsoft.com/source/2026/11/10/ibm-and-microsoft-announce-a-new-ai-agent-platform-designed-for-industry-specific-transformation/)

IBM and Microsoft jointly unveiled an agentic platform for building, running, and governing industry-specific AI agents on November 10. IBM contributes over 6,000 prebuilt agents covering enterprise domains; Microsoft provides the AI, cloud, and security layer via Azure and Copilot infrastructure.

**Why it matters:** A joint IBM-Microsoft platform bridging IBM's enterprise domain expertise and Microsoft's AI infrastructure creates a credible path for regulated industries (finance, healthcare, manufacturing) to adopt agents at scale with governance built in.
