## Research

### AI Decodes DNA Signature of Genetic "Switch" Controlling ~60% of Human Genes
**Source:** [Skycrumbs — AI Research August 2026](https://skycrumbs.com/blog/ai-research-august-2026) · [ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/news/computers_math/artificial_intelligence/)

Researchers trained an AI on roughly 500,000 DNA sequences and identified the initiator element — a genetic "switch" involved in turning genes on — in approximately 60% of human genes. The model can predict how mutations alter this switch, opening a path to understanding the broader grammar of gene regulation. **Why it matters:** Decoding gene-regulation control elements at scale could eventually enable prediction of mutation effects before they are observed in patients, with broad implications for rare-disease diagnosis and precision medicine.

### Model Collapse Enters Enterprise Risk Vocabulary
**Source:** [AI Security and Safety — Model Collapse](https://aisecurityandsafety.org/en/guides/model-collapse/) · [JNGR 5.0 AI Research Papers 2026](https://jngr5.com/jngr/ai-research-papers-2026/)

Research formalising "model collapse" — the degradation that occurs when models train on AI-generated data — is gaining mainstream traction in 2026. The failure has two stages: early variance loss (outputs become less diverse) and late-phase convergence to repetitive or degenerate distributions. Models trained on web data from 2024–2026 are increasingly exposed because that corpus is now substantially AI-generated. **Why it matters:** As AI-generated content saturates the web, labs training on live web data face a structural data-quality problem; mitigation strategies (data provenance, synthetic-data curation, human-validated corpora) are becoming a competitive differentiator.
