## New Model

### 1. Google DeepMind Launches Genie 4 Interactive World Model
**Source:** [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today) · [Lilach Bullock AI News](https://www.lilachbullock.com/ai-news-this-week-for-small-business-22-november-2026/) · Nov 21–22, 2026

Google DeepMind released Genie 4, its most advanced interactive world model to date. Currently available exclusively to Google AI Ultra subscribers, it generates dynamic, navigable environments from text prompts or images. The model builds on the Genie 2 lineage but introduces substantially improved physics simulation and longer coherent scene durations.

**Why it matters:** Genie 4 marks a step toward AI models that don't just describe the world but simulate it interactively — a capability with direct implications for robotics training, game development, and embodied agent research. Keeping it Ultra-only signals Google is treating world modelling as a premium differentiator.

### 2. Microsoft Releases MAI Code 2 and MAI Code 2 Flash
**Source:** [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today) · [Future Tools](https://futuretools.io/news) · Nov 21, 2026

Microsoft released MAI Code 2 and its lighter counterpart MAI Code 2 Flash, enterprise-grade coding models available through Azure AI and GitHub Copilot. The company also announced MAI Licensed Weights, a programme that lets organisations deploy Microsoft AI models in their own Azure environments with dedicated capacity. MAI Code 2 is reported to match or exceed GPT-6.2 Astra on code-specific benchmarks.

**Why it matters:** Microsoft is building a parallel model stack outside its OpenAI partnership, giving enterprise customers a fully controlled, auditable deployment path for coding automation. The Licensed Weights offering is a direct response to enterprise demand for data-residency guarantees.

### 3. Tencent Open-Sources Hunyuan Hy4 Flash
**Source:** [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today) · [LLM Gateway Timeline](https://llmgateway.io/timeline) · Nov 16–21, 2026

Tencent released Hunyuan Hy4 Flash as an open-weight model on Hugging Face. It is a mixture-of-experts (MoE) architecture optimised for fast inference, targeting deployment on consumer-grade hardware. Weights are available under a permissive licence for commercial use.

**Why it matters:** A fast, open MoE model from Tencent adds another competitive option to the rapidly expanding open-weight tier, putting pressure on proprietary API pricing and extending strong multilingual capability (especially Chinese) to self-hosted deployments.

## New Agent Tool

### 4. Google Labs Introduces Stitch Loop Agent
**Source:** [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today) · Nov 21, 2026

Google Labs unveiled Stitch Loop, an agentic tool that connects to a GitHub codebase and a live metrics dashboard to identify correlations between code changes and performance regressions. Stitch Loop can open draft PRs with proposed fixes and annotates them with the specific metric anomaly that triggered the action. It is in limited preview for Workspace Business and Enterprise customers.

**Why it matters:** Stitch Loop closes a feedback loop that engineers typically manage manually — linking deploys to metric shifts. By giving the agent both code context and production telemetry, Google is demonstrating a path toward agents that reason about software quality end-to-end rather than in isolation.

## New Framework

### 5. IBM and Microsoft's "Imagine Enterprise" Agentic Platform Now Available
**Source:** [IBM Newsroom](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-11-10-ibm-and-microsoft-announce-a-new-ai-agent-platform-designed-for-industry-specific-transformation) · [Microsoft Source](https://news.microsoft.com/source/2026/11/10/ibm-and-microsoft-announce-a-new-ai-agent-platform-designed-for-industry-specific-transformation/) · Nov 10–22, 2026

IBM and Microsoft's jointly developed "Imagine Enterprise" platform — announced November 10 — entered general availability this week. It provides a full lifecycle environment for building, running, and governing industry-specific AI agents. IBM Consulting ships more than 6,000 pre-built agents and assets across sectors including financial services, manufacturing, and healthcare. The platform runs on Azure and integrates with IBM watsonx.

**Why it matters:** Imagine Enterprise is one of the most comprehensive joint enterprise AI offerings from two major legacy tech vendors. Its pre-built agent library reduces time-to-deployment for regulated industries that cannot afford greenfield agent development.

## Research

### 6. LINEAGE Benchmark: Retrieval of Research Lineage from AI Literature
**Source:** [ArXiv Agents Radar Digest 2026-11-20](https://github.com/kouweizhu/agents-radar/issues/1046) · arXiv 2611.xxxxx · Nov 20, 2026

Researchers released LINEAGE, a benchmark for evaluating how well AI systems can trace the empirical ancestry of a given research result — identifying which prior papers contributed specific datasets, baselines, or experimental protocols. The benchmark covers 1,200 paper-lineage pairs across NLP, computer vision, and RL, and finds that current retrieval-augmented models struggle significantly on lineage attribution compared to keyword search.

**Why it matters:** As AI-assisted research becomes standard practice, correctly attributing scientific credit and tracking result provenance is essential for reproducibility. LINEAGE directly addresses the accountability gap in automated literature review tools.

### 7. Paper Challenges the "LLMs Are Declining" Narrative
**Source:** [ArXiv AI Research Digest 2026-11-11](https://github.com/datnguyenquy94/news-radar/issues/1043) · arXiv 2611.xxxxx · Nov 11, 2026

A new paper argues that widespread reports of LLM capability decline are an artefact of rising user expectations and harder prompt distributions rather than model regressions. The authors construct a controlled longitudinal benchmark and show that the same model evaluated on matched prompts performs consistently over time, while user-submitted prompts have grown systematically more complex.

**Why it matters:** This reframing has practical implications for model evaluation methodology. If the "decline" signal is primarily sociological rather than technical, benchmark design needs to account for user-distribution drift — a problem that becomes acute as models are updated and user bases grow.

### 9. Amazon Reportedly Nearing $7.5B Acquisition of Decart
**Source:** [StartupHub AI Funding](https://www.startuphub.ai/ai-news/funding-roundup/2026/this-week-in-startup-funding) · [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today) · Nov 21, 2026

Amazon is reportedly close to acquiring Israeli AI chip-switching startup Decart in a deal valued at approximately $7.5 billion. Decart builds inference switching technology that dynamically routes workloads between hardware accelerators to reduce cost and latency. An announcement could come at AWS re:Invent on November 30.

**Why it matters:** A Decart acquisition would give Amazon proprietary inference optimisation at the chip level, strengthening AWS's position against Google TPUs and Microsoft-backed Maia. It signals that the next infrastructure war is at the routing layer, not just the chip layer.

### 10. Trump Signals Penalties for US AI Companies Building Data Centers Abroad
**Source:** [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today) · [Future Tools](https://futuretools.io/news) · Nov 21, 2026

President Trump said the administration is considering measures to penalise US AI companies that build data centres in foreign countries, framing the issue as a matter of national security and economic competitiveness. No specific legislation has been drafted, and the statement came as part of broader remarks about US technology policy.

**Why it matters:** Even as a signal without teeth, this puts US hyperscalers and AI labs on notice that international data centre expansion — a primary strategy for meeting power demands and skirting US grid constraints — may face political headwinds. It could accelerate domestic data centre investment and reshape near-term capex plans across the sector.
