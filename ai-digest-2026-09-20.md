## 7. Research · ReDeck: Render-Grounded Refinement for Document-to-Slide Generation

**Source:** [arXiv — Artificial Intelligence Sep 2026](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/new)

ReDeck introduces a step-level, render-grounded refinement approach for automatically converting documents into presentation slides. Rather than generating a slide deck in a single pass, ReDeck iteratively renders intermediate outputs and uses visual feedback to correct layout, typography, and content coherence at each step. The approach addresses a persistent weakness of LLM-based slide generation: models that can write good slide text but produce visually incoherent or structurally broken decks.
