## New Model

### 1. OpenAI Introduces GPT-6.1 Sol
**Source:** [AI Updates Today — llm-stats.com](https://llm-stats.com/llm-updates)

OpenAI launched GPT-6.1 Sol, delivering near-Astra-level intelligence at one-fifth the price — billed as "the most cost-efficient model for its performance available today." The release comes the same week OpenAI postponed its GPT-6.1 Astra launch after concerning internal safety evaluation results.

**Why it matters:** Cost efficiency at frontier capability is a recurring competitive pressure point; Sol could pressure incumbents like Anthropic and Google to reprice their own models.

## New Agent Tool

### 3. OpenAI Launches "dots" — Always-On Personal AI Agent

## Research

### 6. "Safeguarding LLM Agents against Long-Horizon Threats via Shadow Memory" — ACM CCS 2026
**Source:** [arXiv / DailyArXiv](https://github.com/somewordstoolate/DailyArXiv/issues/336)

Accepted to ACM CCS 2026, this paper introduces "Shadow Memory" — a security mechanism that monitors LLM agent behavior across long-horizon tasks to detect and block subtle threat vectors that only manifest after many steps.

**Why it matters:** Security for long-running agents is an emerging blind spot; formalizing defenses at the ACM CCS level signals the research community is taking it seriously.
