# AI News Digest — 2026-10-17

## New Model

### 1. Meta Releases Muse Spark Open — 816B Open-Weight Model
**Source:** [Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/meta-releases-muse-spark-to-reenter-ai-race-273982bf) · [Pasquale Pillitteri](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/24330/meta-muse-spark-open-816b-open-weights)

Meta released the open-weight version of its Muse Spark model on October 15, making the 816B-parameter (42B active) model available on Hugging Face under the Muse Model License. The model targets coding and multi-step agent tasks, supports up to 1M tokens of context, and can run locally on two linked Apple M5 Ultra Mac Studios via MLX.

**Why it matters:** This is Meta's strongest open-weight release and its most direct challenge to Anthropic and OpenAI in the agent workload space. Open weights at this scale lower the barrier for enterprises to run frontier-class models on-premise.

### 2. Google Gemini 4 Argon Now Broadly Available
**Source:** [AI Agent Store — Week of Oct 15](https://aiagentstore.ai/ai-agent-news/this-week)

Google has opened Gemini 4 Argon — previously restricted to vetted cyber defenders — to general availability, pricing it at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. The model is optimized for long-running agent tasks.

**Why it matters:** Argon's general release brings Google's most powerful frontier model into the hands of general developers for the first time, intensifying competition in the agentic reasoning tier.

## New Agent Tool

### 4. Workday Agent Harness Goes GA with Sana as Primary Interface
**Source:** [AI Agents News — Week of Oct 15](https://aiagentstore.ai/ai-agent-news/this-week)

Workday has made its Agent Harness generally available and named Sana as the default agent interface across HR, finance, and IT workflows. The platform includes memory, guardrails, regional data controls, model choice, evaluations, and step-by-step execution traces.

**Why it matters:** Enterprise agent infrastructure is entering a consolidation phase; Workday GA-ing this platform means large organizations now have a production-ready, auditable layer for deploying AI agents inside regulated workflows.

### 5. American Express Introduces "Intent Intelligence Framework" for Agentic Commerce
**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/13/amex-introduces-a-framework-to-prevent-ai-agents-from-making-unauthorized-purchases/)

Amex unveiled a framework that uses verified user intent signals to prevent AI shopping agents from making purchases a user did not explicitly authorize. The company says it is already in discussions with partners across the payments ecosystem.

**Why it matters:** As agentic commerce scales, financial authorization frameworks become critical infrastructure. Amex's move sets a precedent for how payment networks may govern AI-initiated transactions.

## Research

### 7. RSI-Anything: Recursive Self-Improvement via Predictive World Models
**Source:** [Hugging Face Trending Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers/trending)

A paper dated October 9 introduces RSI-Anything, which uses predictive world models to enable recursive self-improvement (RSI) across coding, UI navigation, lab planning, and scientific optimization tasks.

**Why it matters:** RSI has long been a theoretical milestone in AI; empirical results across multiple diverse domains in a single framework suggest the field is moving closer to systems that can autonomously improve their own capabilities.

---

### 8. Berkeley Study: 10 Minutes of AI Use Erodes Persistence on Hard Tasks
**Source:** [Berkeley News](https://news.berkeley.edu/2026/10/09/using-ai-for-just-10-minutes-erodes-your-ability-to-persist-at-hard-things/)

A peer-reviewed study presented at the Conference on Language Modeling found that participants who used an AI tool for just 10 minutes while working on math and reasoning problems subsequently showed significantly reduced ability to focus and persist on difficult tasks.

**Why it matters:** This is one of the first rigorous studies of AI's cognitive side-effects on human capability. It has immediate implications for educational AI policy and enterprise deployment guidelines.
