### 5. Amazon Retires Bedrock Agents, Transitions to AgentCore
**Source:** [AWS News Blog](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/introducing-amazon-bedrock-agentcore-securely-deploy-and-operate-ai-agents-at-any-scale) | [Enterprise DNA](https://enterprisedna.co/resources/news/amazon-bedrock-agents-classic-agentcore-enterprise-july-2026/)

As of July 30, Amazon closed Bedrock Agents to new customers, renaming it Bedrock Agents Classic. The replacement, Amazon Bedrock AgentCore, is architected from the ground up for multi-agent systems with delegation, collaboration, and shared state — a fundamentally different model from Classic's single-agent sequential approach.

**Why it matters:** Teams building on Bedrock Agents must plan a migration; more broadly, this signals that AWS views the multi-agent coordination layer as the central abstraction for enterprise AI workloads going forward.

## Research

### 6. DeepMind: "Reasoning Collapse" Under Problem Reformulation
**Source:** [Skycrumbs — AI Research August 2026](https://skycrumbs.com/blog/ai-research-august-2026) | [arXiv RAGEN-2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.06268)

A DeepMind team published research identifying "reasoning collapse" — where LLMs that solve a problem in one framing fail when the same problem is presented differently — and proposed a training methodology that reduces this brittleness by approximately 40% on their benchmark.

**Why it matters:** Reasoning collapse is a reliability flaw with direct consequences for deployed agents that handle varied user phrasing; a 40% reduction in brittleness is a meaningful step toward production-safe reasoning systems.

---

### 7. DeepMind: Hallucination Reduction in Vision-Language Models
**Source:** [Skycrumbs — AI Research August 2026](https://skycrumbs.com/blog/ai-research-august-2026)

A separate DeepMind team published a method for significantly reducing factual errors in vision-language models (VLMs), targeting the hallucination problem in multimodal generation.

**Why it matters:** VLM hallucinations are a key blocker for production deployments — agentic systems that interpret images or diagrams cannot be trusted when they confabulate. This technique could unlock more reliable vision-grounded agents.

### 8. EverMind State-of-Agent-Memory Benchmark — August 2026 Update
**Source:** [EverMind Blog](https://evermind.ai/blogs/top-ai-memory-systems-benchmarked-in-2026) | [Mem0 State of Agent Memory 2026](https://mem0.ai/blog/state-of-ai-agent-memory-2026)

EverMind published August benchmark results showing 90.04% recall on HaluMem, alongside performance on LoCoMo, LongMemEval, and PersonaMem v2. The accompanying state-of-the-field report covers 21 frameworks and 20 vector stores across managed cloud, open-source self-hosted, and local MCP hosting models.

**Why it matters:** Memory infrastructure is becoming a critical differentiator for production agents — this benchmark gives teams an objective comparison across the now-crowded field of dedicated memory systems (Mem0, Zep, Letta, and others).
