## New Model

### 1. Google Gemini 4 Hydrogen
**Source:** [LLM Gateway Timeline](https://llmgateway.io/timeline) · [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-friday-november-13-2026/)

Google released Gemini 4 Hydrogen on November 13, the latest in the Gemini 4 family, with a focus on a more conversational Search experience. It follows Gemini 4 Carbon released earlier in the month.

**Why it matters:** Gemini 4 Hydrogen deepens Google's integration of frontier LLMs directly into Search, pushing conversational AI further into everyday information retrieval.

---

### 2. Anthropic Claude Fable 5.2 & Mythos 5.2
**Source:** [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today) · [LLM Gateway Timeline](https://llmgateway.io/timeline)

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 (November 9) and Mythos 5.2, the latter aimed at coding and scientific research tasks. The release extends the Claude 5 family with higher capability tiers.

**Why it matters:** Continued rapid iteration from Anthropic signals an accelerating model cadence; Mythos 5.2's science and coding focus reflects increasing specialization within model families.

---

### 3. DeepSeek Opens App Features as APIs
**Source:** [The AI Break](https://theaibreak.substack.com/p/dont-miss-out-openais-major-announcement/comments) · [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/)

DeepSeek opened its app-level capabilities — including web search, OCR, and voice — as developer APIs. This makes DeepSeek's distinctive features programmatically accessible for the first time.

**Why it matters:** Lowering the barrier to build on DeepSeek's search and document-understanding capabilities could spur a new wave of open-weight powered applications.

## New Agent Tool

### 4. OpenAI ChatGPT Wallet — Agentic Payments
**Source:** [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/digest/everything-that-happened-in-ai-today-friday-november-13-2026/) · [AI News Today](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)

OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet, allowing AI agents to make purchases on behalf of users with explicit approval. The feature launches with Visa, Mastercard, and Amex support.

**Why it matters:** This is a concrete step toward agentic commerce — AI agents that can complete real-world transactions — marking a shift from AI as advisor to AI as actor in financial workflows.

---

### 5. CopilotKit OpenBot 1.0
**Source:** [CASRAI](https://casrai.org/news/copilotkit-openbot-1-0-agent-workspace-launch-2026)

Released November 10, OpenBot 1.0 is a self-hostable, composable workspace for running AI agents with built-in human approval steps and administrator controls. It is fully open-source.

**Why it matters:** OpenBot offers teams an on-premise alternative to managed agent clouds, addressing enterprise needs around data sovereignty and audit trails.

---

### 6. Linux Foundation Agent Name Service (ANS)
**Source:** [AI Agents Directory](https://aiagentsdirectory.com/news/ai-agents-news-brief-november-11-2026)

The Linux Foundation launched the Agent Name Service — a DNS-anchored, certificate-based identity registry for AI agents. Eleven organizations signed on as early supporters.

**Why it matters:** As multi-agent systems proliferate, trust and identity between agents becomes critical infrastructure; ANS is an attempt to standardize how agents verify one another.

---

### 7. AWS Bedrock AgentCore + Universal Agent Evaluation Framework
**Source:** [AI Agents Directory](https://aiagentsdirectory.com/news/ai-agents-news-brief-november-13-2026)

AWS released an updated Bedrock AgentCore runtime with hardware-isolated sessions and usage-based billing for long-running agent tasks, alongside a new Universal Agent Evaluation Framework (UAEF) for cross-framework agent assessment.

**Why it matters:** Hardware isolation and standardized evaluation are two of the remaining production blockers for deploying agents at scale; AWS shipping both together signals enterprise readiness is a priority.

## Research

### 8. Google Antigravity / Teamwork — Multi-Agent Math System Released to Researchers
**Source:** [AI Agents News Weekly](https://aiagentstore.ai/ai-agent-news/this-week)

Google's internal multi-agent system "Antigravity/Teamwork," previously used with Gemini models to tackle open math problems, is being released to external researchers. The system uses dedicated falsifier agents to verify candidate solutions and partitions context to maintain focus.

**Why it matters:** Releasing a production-proven multi-agent reasoning system to researchers could accelerate progress on formal verification and long-horizon scientific problem-solving.

## Other

### 9. LangChain Raises $300M at $3B Valuation; LangSmith Hits 7,000 Customers
**Source:** [AI News Today](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today) · [bcombinator](https://bcombinator.com/en/2026/11/12/whats-happening-in-tech-this-weeks-deals-and-trends/)

### 10. UK Budget: £165M National AI Biosecurity Initiative
**Source:** [TLT's AI Brief](https://www.tlt.com/insights-and-events/insight/tlts-ai-brief-november-2026) · [InfoTech Roundup](https://www.infotech.com/software-reviews/vendor-technology-notes/big-5-ai-vendor-roundup-week-of-november-2-2026)

The UK government's latest budget includes a £165M National AI Biosecurity Initiative, anchored by an AI Biosecurity Lab backed by £100M in public funding and up to £200M in commitments from OpenAI and Anthropic.

**Why it matters:** Government-backed AI biosecurity infrastructure — with direct involvement from leading labs — sets a precedent for how nations may co-invest with industry to manage catastrophic AI risk.
