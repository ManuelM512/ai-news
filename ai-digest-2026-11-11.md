## Research

### 7. FP4 Pretraining Slows Acquisition of Rare Facts
**Source:** [ArXiv AI Research Digest 2026-11-11 (HuggerHustle/ai-radar)](https://github.com/HuggerHustle/ai-radar/issues/203)

Paper: *"What the Loss Curve Misses: FP4 Pretraining Slows the Acquisition of Rare Facts"* (arXiv 2611.12832). Researchers find that training in 4-bit floating point reduces model accuracy on rare or long-tail facts even when perplexity and standard benchmarks look fine. **Why it matters:** As FP4/FP8 training becomes a default cost-saving measure, this finding warns that standard evals may miss systematic knowledge gaps, particularly for niche domains where factual reliability is critical.

---

### 8. META-IR: Introspective Reasoning for Adaptive Safety Alignment
**Source:** [ArXiv Listing cs.AI — November 10](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/current)

META-IR introduces an introspective reasoning mechanism for LLMs that enables adaptive safety alignment — allowing models to revise safety judgments in context rather than applying static rules. **Why it matters:** Static alignment rules fail under adversarial prompting and edge cases; dynamic introspective methods could make safety behaviors more robust and generalizable without sacrificing helpfulness.
