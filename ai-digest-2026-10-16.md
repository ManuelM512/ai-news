## Research

**DeepMind AlphaProof Nexus Solves 9 Erdős Problems**
- **Source:** [36Kr / Science journal](https://eu.36kr.com/en/p/4028160975999625)
- **Summary:** DeepMind's AlphaProof Nexus reportedly solved 9 long-standing unsolved mathematical problems first posed by Paul Erdős, with findings published in *Science* around October 8. The system autonomously identified and proved results that had stumped mathematicians for over five decades.
- **Why it matters:** Represents a landmark step for AI in formal reasoning and mathematics, extending beyond theorem-proving benchmarks to genuine open-problem discovery.

**UCLA Light-Based Deepfake Detection at 98% Accuracy**
- **Source:** [ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/news/computers_math/artificial_intelligence/)
