## Research

### 3. OpenAI's Astra Solves Ten Open Mathematics Problems
**Source:** [SiliconAngle](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/02/openais-astra-solves-10-long-open-math-problems-publishes-proofs/) · [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/jonmarkman/2026/08/03/openais-astra-solved-10-decades-old-math-problems-for-just-2000/)

OpenAI revealed that an internal version of **Astra** — its next-generation multi-agent model — solved ten open problems across mathematics and theoretical computer science (group theory, coding theory, quantum complexity, cryptography, and more) for approximately $2,000 in API tokens. The headline result is the first explicit construction of a **non-sofic group**, an open question since Gromov introduced the concept in 1999. All proofs were formalized in Lean 4 and published as a 249-page manuscript collection. Fields Medal winner Timothy Gowers said he would recommend one proof for a top journal without hesitation.

**Why it matters:** AI doing genuine frontier mathematics at journal quality for trivial cost is a landmark moment — not a benchmark, but actual unsolved problems. Astra itself remains unreleased.

## New Agent Tool

### 4. Amazon Deprecates Bedrock Agents Classic; AgentCore Becomes the Standard
**Source:** [AWS Blog](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/new-in-amazon-bedrock-agentcore-build-agents-with-broader-knowledge-and-continuous-learning/) · [ServerGurus](https://servergurus.com/blog/aws-bedrock-agentcore-2026)

As of July 30, 2026, Amazon Bedrock Agents Classic is closed to new customers. **Amazon Bedrock AgentCore** is now the designated successor — a framework-agnostic platform covering runtime, managed identity, persistent memory, a unified tool gateway, policy controls, and end-to-end observability. It works with CrewAI, LangGraph, LlamaIndex, Google ADK, and OpenAI Agents SDK, as well as any model in or outside Amazon Bedrock.

**Why it matters:** AWS is forcing a migration to a richer, more opinionated agent infrastructure stack; teams still on Bedrock Agents Classic need a migration plan now.

## Other

### 5. EU AI Act High-Risk Provisions Now Enforceable
**Source:** [European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-starts-enforcing-ai-act-rules-and-new-transparency-requirements-2-august) · [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/08/02/eu-rules-on-ai-models-become-enforceable-whats-going-to-change)

The EU AI Act's high-risk provisions — risk management systems, human oversight, conformity assessments — became enforceable on August 2, 2026. Penalties reach €35M or 7% of global annual turnover. Chatbots, AI agents, and digital-human systems must now clearly disclose they are AI. Only 8 of 27 EU member states have designated required national competent authorities, raising concerns about uneven enforcement.

**Why it matters:** The world's first comprehensive AI law is now live with real teeth; every global AI product touching EU users is now in scope for compliance obligations.

### 6. White House AI Safety Meeting: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Invited
**Source:** [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-03/openai-anthropic-google-to-join-white-house-ai-safety-meeting)

The Trump administration invited OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google to a White House meeting to discuss a new US framework for voluntary AI safety testing, stemming from a June executive order on AI cybersecurity. **Why it matters:** The US-EU regulatory divergence is now in sharp relief; the voluntary vs. mandatory split will shape how companies build and deploy models globally.
