## New Model

### 1. Google Gemini 4 Carbon Rolls Out to General Public
**Source:** [letsdatascience.com](https://letsdatascience.com/news/google-rolls-out-gemini-4-carbon-to-general-public-9b41bcb4) · [9to5google.com](https://9to5google.com/?p=731557)

**Why it matters:** Carbon's general rollout extends Google's latest generation to all developers and enterprise customers, closing the gap with OpenAI and Anthropic in the flagship model tier.

### 2. Mistral Large 4 Open Weights Released ("Le Chonk")
**Source:** [mistral.ai](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4-oss/) · [letsdatascience.com](https://letsdatascience.com/news/mistral-releases-open-weights-for-1-trillion-parameter-mistr-d712eb0e)

Mistral released the open weights for Mistral Large 4 on October 29, three weeks after its API-only preview. The model is a multimodal mixture-of-experts architecture with ~1 trillion total parameters and ~52 billion active per token. Weights are published under the Mistral Ecosystem Open-Weight license on Hugging Face (`mistralai/Mistral-Large-4-1T-A52B`).

**Why it matters:** A 1-trillion-parameter open-weight flagship from Mistral is a significant addition to the open-source ecosystem, giving organizations an alternative to closed commercial models for coding, reasoning, and security tasks.

## New Agent Tool

### 5. IBM + Microsoft "Imagine Enterprise" Agentic Platform
**Source:** [news.microsoft.com](https://news.microsoft.com/source/2026/11/10/ibm-and-microsoft-announce-a-new-ai-agent-platform-designed-for-industry-specific-transformation/)

IBM and Microsoft jointly announced Imagine Enterprise on November 10, an agentic AI platform for industry-specific business process automation. IBM Consulting contributes a fleet of over 6,000 AI agents and assets from IBM Consulting Advantage; Microsoft provides the underlying infrastructure and Azure integration.

**Why it matters:** A major enterprise vendor partnership combining two agent ecosystems into a single governed platform signals that enterprise AI procurement is consolidating around multi-vendor agentic offerings.

### 6. AWS Bedrock AgentCore Runtime Update (Nov 12)
**Source:** [aiagentsdirectory.com](https://aiagentsdirectory.com/news/ai-agents-news-brief-november-13-2026)

AWS updated Bedrock AgentCore with hardware-isolated session execution and usage-based billing for long-duration agentic tasks. The update targets workloads where agents must persist context across hours or days without shared infrastructure risk.

**Why it matters:** Hardened session isolation and consumption billing make long-running agents more practical and cost-transparent for regulated industries.

### 7. Linux Foundation Agent Name Service (ANS)
**Source:** [aiagentsdirectory.com](https://aiagentsdirectory.com/news/ai-agents-news-brief-november-11-2026)

The Linux Foundation announced an Agent Name Service: a DNS-anchored registry that uses domain records and certificate-based signals to register, publish, and verify AI agent identities. It is positioned as open infrastructure for cross-organization agent trust.

**Why it matters:** As multi-agent systems proliferate, identity and verification become critical attack surfaces; an open standard from a neutral body could become foundational infrastructure.

## New Framework

### 8. Google ADK 2.0 — Graph-Based Multi-Agent Orchestration
**Source:** [aiagentsdirectory.com](https://aiagentsdirectory.com/news/ai-agents-news-brief-november-11-2026) · [shakudo.io](https://www.shakudo.io/blog/top-9-ai-agent-frameworks)

Google's Agent Development Kit 2.0, announced at Google I/O 2026 and updated in November, shifts from a linear execution model to a graph-based execution engine. It natively supports multi-agent orchestration, allowing developers to wire agents as nodes in a DAG.

**Why it matters:** A graph-based model enables more complex, conditional agent workflows without custom glue code, and ADK's integration with Vertex AI makes it the natural path for Google Cloud-centric teams.

## Research

### 9. ReTrace: Tool-Using Agents Without Planners (arXiv 2611.14492)
**Source:** [github.com/datnguyenquy94/news-radar](https://github.com/datnguyenquy94/news-radar/issues/1043)

ReTrace is a technique that distills the behavior of tool-using agents (which typically rely on explicit planner modules) into compact, planner-free workflows. The approach aims to reduce latency and inference cost by eliminating the separate planning pass.

**Why it matters:** Planner overhead is a significant efficiency bottleneck in agentic pipelines; a distillation path that preserves capability without the planner could meaningfully reduce production costs.

---

### 10. "Exo: An Architecture for Self-Improving Systems at Runtime" (arXiv 2611.00463)
**Source:** [arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/current](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/current)

Exo, authored by a team including Ion Stoica and Martin Casado, proposes an architecture where AI systems can modify their own inference behavior at runtime based on feedback, without requiring retraining. It targets continual adaptation in long-lived deployment.

**Why it matters:** Runtime self-improvement is one of the more speculative but high-impact areas of AI systems research; a credible architecture paper from prominent systems researchers will likely draw significant attention.

### 11. Nvidia Reportedly Acquiring Hugging Face for ~$13B
**Source:** [pricepertoken.com/news/funding](https://pricepertoken.com/funding) · [superpowerdaily.com](https://superpowerdaily.com/signals/funding)

Multiple funding trackers report that Nvidia is acquiring Hugging Face at a valuation of approximately $12.9–13B. The deal has not been confirmed by either company. Hugging Face is the dominant open-source model and dataset hosting platform, with over 1 million models on its hub.

**Why it matters:** If confirmed, this would be one of the largest AI acquisitions to date and would put the central hub of the open-source AI ecosystem under hardware vendor control — a structurally significant shift.
