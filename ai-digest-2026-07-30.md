## Other — Policy / Safety

### 1. "Pacing the Frontier" — 1,178 AI Workers Call for International Development Slowdown Tools
**Source:** [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-28/openai-anthropic-staff-share-letter-asking-us-to-help-pace-ai-progress) · [CNN Business](https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/28/tech/ai-development-tech-employees-open-letter) · [Results Sense](https://www.resultsense.com/news/2026-07-29-pacing-the-frontier-employee-statement/)

On July 28, 1,178 AI researchers and engineers from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, and Meta — including Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei, OpenAI Chief Scientist Jakub Pachocki, and Meta AI Chief Scientist Shengjia Zhao — signed a joint letter titled "Pacing the Frontier." The letter urges the U.S. government to develop the technical and governance infrastructure needed to verifiably slow AI development if it advances faster than human oversight can safely manage. Both OpenAI and Anthropic subsequently endorsed the statement on behalf of their companies.

**Why it matters:** This is the first time senior leadership from competing frontier labs have jointly signed a public call for a potential slowdown mechanism — and one with a concrete ask: build the tools so the option exists, before it's needed. The signatories are not fringe safety advocates but the research leads who build these systems.

## New Agent Tool

### 2. Cyabra Launches Coordinated Activity Detection Agent
**Source:** [AIThority](https://aithority.com/machine-learning/cyabra-launches-ai-agent-that-automatically-investigates-coordinated-online-activity-and-delivers-a-verdict/) · [Cyabra Blog](https://cyabra.com/blog/introducing-cyabras-coordinated-activity-detection/)

Cyabra released Coordinated Activity Detection on July 28, an AI agent that autonomously applies the company's analyst methodology to determine whether online activity reflects coordinated inauthentic behavior — and delivers a single, evidence-backed verdict per scan. The system outputs one of four states (Confirmed Coordination, Likely Coordination, Insufficient Evidence, or No Coordination Detected) with a confidence score and an evidentiary report, typically in under 30 seconds. It is the first in a planned series of investigative AI agents, with narrative analysis and influence mapping to follow.

**Why it matters:** Manual investigation of coordinated inauthentic behavior at scale is slow and labor-intensive. Automating the verdict step — not just the signal aggregation — pushes this capability from analyst teams to any organization running social media monitoring at volume.

## Other — Infrastructure

### 3. AMD Locks In 530 MW of Core Scientific Data Center Capacity in $14B Deal
**Source:** [Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4619263-amd-partners-with-core-scientific-for-500-mw-ai-infrastructure-deal) · [Cloud Computing News](https://www.cloudcomputing-news.net/news/amd-core-scientific-data-centre-deal/) · [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/409887/core-scientific-ties-ai-pivot-amd-multi-gigawatt-infrastructure-deal)

AMD and Core Scientific announced a partnership on July 28 under which AMD secures approximately 530 MW of data center capacity across five U.S. sites — in Texas, Georgia, Oklahoma, and Alabama — via 15-year lease agreements. The deal carries more than $14 billion in potential base contracted revenue and includes joint collaboration on infrastructure design and deployment of AMD Instinct GPUs, EPYC CPUs, and ROCm software. Full capacity is expected online by end of 2028.

**Why it matters:** AMD is building large-scale, owned data center access rather than relying on cloud partners — a direct infrastructure play against Nvidia's dominant position and a signal that the AI infrastructure arms race is no longer just for hyperscalers.

## Other — Regulation

### 4. EU Digital Omnibus on AI Enters Into Force
**Source:** [Hunton Privacy Blog](https://www.hunton.com/privacy-and-cybersecurity-law-blog/eu-digital-omnibus-on-ai-enters-into-force) · [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/321681/20260727/eu-ai-act-omnibus-law-six-days-transparency-deadline-nudifier-apps-banned-december.htm) · [Lewis Silkin](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2026/07/27/the-digital-omnibus-on-ai-enters-into-force-today-102nedo)

Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 — the Digital Omnibus on AI — entered into legal force on July 27, three days after publication in the Official Journal. The omnibus preserves the AI Act's existing Article 5 prohibitions and Article 50 transparency rules on their original schedules but pushes full compliance for most high-risk Annex III systems to December 2027 and for Annex I embedded systems to August 2028. Two new Article 5 prohibitions take effect December 2026: non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII) generation and AI-generated CSAM.

**Why it matters:** The deadline extensions ease near-term compliance pressure for companies selling high-risk AI in Europe, but the new NCII and CSAM bans land in five months — a hard deadline for any provider operating consumer-facing generative models in the EU.

## Other — Robotics / Industry

### 5. Agility Robotics to Go Public at $2.5B Valuation via SPAC Merger
**Source:** [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/articles/agility-robotics-goes-public-churchill-140537469.html) · [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/agility-robotics-files-for-spac-merger-with-churchill-xi-93CH-4791307) · [Stock Titan](https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/CCXI/425-churchill-capital-corp-xi-business-combination-communication-c26fddc4b0a9.html)

Agility Robotics announced it will go public by merging with Churchill Capital Corp XI, valuing the company at $2.5 billion pre-money. Foxconn is leading the PIPE alongside Churchill XI's $420 million trust account, for expected gross proceeds exceeding $620 million. Agility will list under the ticker AGLT and has secured more than $300 million in multi-year orders for Digit v5, its next-generation humanoid designed for shared human-robot environments. Backers include Amazon, Nvidia, and SoftBank.

**Why it matters:** The SPAC path to public markets gives Agility a large capital injection as the humanoid robotics race intensifies — and the $300M+ order book for Digit v5 provides concrete demand evidence that differentiates this listing from earlier robotics SPACs that were pre-revenue.

## Research

### 6. OpenAI Analysis of 800,000+ ChatGPT Work Messages Finds AI Crossing Functional Boundaries
**Source:** [Build Fast with AI](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-july-29-2026) · [John Sviokla Substack](https://johnsviokla.substack.com/p/ep-630-daily-ai-news-july-29-2026)

OpenAI analyzed more than 800,000 work-related ChatGPT messages and found that employees are increasingly using AI across functional boundaries — finance workers doing marketing analysis, engineers drafting communications, and so on. Analysts cited in coverage noted the data points toward a structural shift: smaller, AI-augmented teams handling the output previously requiring larger departmental headcounts.

**Why it matters:** Large-scale behavioral data on how knowledge workers actually use AI — as opposed to how firms say they deploy it — is rare. The cross-functional usage pattern, if it persists, has material implications for organizational design and labor demand well beyond the white-collar roles currently most discussed in AI displacement debates.
