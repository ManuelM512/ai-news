## New Model

### 1. Google Releases Gemini 3.8 Flash with Security Sibling
**Source:** [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/09/02/with-gemini-38-flash-google-reminds-everyone-its-still-in-the-race/5294049) · [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/09/02/gemini-3-8-flash-launch/)

Google launched Gemini 3.8 Flash on September 2 — its third Flash release in six weeks — priced at $0.75/$3.75 per 1M tokens with a 1M context window, alongside a locked-down security sibling called Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber. The rapid iteration pace signals Google is fighting hard for developer mindshare in the fast/cheap tier against competitors.

### 2. Anthropic Fable 5.1 Goes GA
**Source:** [LLM Stats](https://llm-stats.com/llm-updates) · [LLM Gateway](https://llmgateway.io/timeline)

Anthropic's Fable 5.1 reached general availability on September 1, keeping the same $10/$50 per 1M token pricing as Fable 5 while dropping cache read costs to $0.25 and posting a Terminal-Bench-Science score of 52.6 — more than double Fable 5's 24.7. A trusted-access twin, Mythos 5.1, was released simultaneously for higher-sensitivity workloads.

### 3. Qwen3.8 27B Released by Alibaba (Airside)
**Source:** [LLM Gateway Timeline](https://llmgateway.io/timeline)

Alibaba's Airside division released Qwen3.8 27B on September 2, continuing the Qwen family's relentless cadence of open-weight releases. The model targets the mid-range parameter tier where open-source models increasingly compete with proprietary alternatives on price-to-performance.

## New Agent Tool

### 4. Microsoft Agent Framework Ships v1.16.0 and .NET 1.20.0
**Source:** [Microsoft DevBlogs](https://devblogs.microsoft.com/agent-framework/) · [Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/microsoft)

Microsoft Agent Framework released two updates this week: Python v1.16.0 adds configurable first-task wait timeouts and richer OpenTelemetry/OTLP setup, while .NET v1.20.0 ships hosted workflow improvements, better cancellation and logprob handling, stronger Cosmos DB reliability, and a new Mem0Sharp sample integration. The framework, which consolidated Semantic Kernel and AutoGen at GA in April, continues its rapid iteration cadence.

### 5. AccuKnox Launches AgentZ Production Platform
**Source:** [AI Agent Store](https://aiagentstore.ai/ai-agent-news/this-week)

AccuKnox released AgentZ, a model-agnostic platform that bundles agents, sandboxes, workflows, role-based access control, runtime credential injection, and audit traces in a single deployment target supporting SaaS, on-prem, and air-gapped environments. It addresses the gap between agent prototypes and production-grade deployment that most frameworks still leave unresolved.

## Research

### 6. OpenAI's Astra Surpasses Preparedness 'Critical' Cybersecurity Threshold
**Source:** [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today) · [Mean.CEO](https://blog.mean.ceo/open-ai-news-september-2026/)

OpenAI announced that Astra is the first LLM to exceed its internal Preparedness Framework's "Critical" cybersecurity threshold, scoring a perfect ExploitBench and autonomously discovering and exploiting two zero-day vulnerabilities in modified test environments. The company plans to release Astra "soon" but will gate its most advanced offensive cyber capabilities — raising immediate debate about disclosure norms for dual-use AI.

## Other

### 7. Cohere and Aleph Alpha Announce Merger
**Source:** [Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/global-startup-exits-ipo-ma-soar-ai-q2-h1-2026/) · [AI Funding Tracker](https://aifundingtracker.com/ai-startup-funding-news-today/)

Cohere (Canada) and Aleph Alpha (Germany) announced plans to merge, forming a transatlantic sovereign enterprise AI provider anchored outside of U.S. hyperscalers. Schwarz Group (Lidl's parent) committed $600 million in structured financing for Cohere's Series E to fund the deal. The consolidation signals increasing demand for non-U.S.-domiciled enterprise AI options amid data sovereignty regulations.

### 8. Nebius Acquires Eigen AI for ~$643M
**Source:** [AI M&A Insider](https://theaiinsider.tech/2026/07/22/ai-ma-in-2026-who-is-acquiring-whom/) · [AI Funding Tracker](https://aifundingtracker.com/ai-startup-funding-news-today/)

Nebius (NASDAQ: NBIS) agreed to acquire Eigen AI, a specialist in inference optimization and model compression, for approximately $643 million in cash and stock. The deal is expected to close in the coming weeks and positions Nebius to offer vertically integrated inference infrastructure at reduced compute cost.

### 9. ChatGPT Enterprise Adds Healthcare Plugins for HIPAA Workspaces
**Source:** [Releasebot – OpenAI](https://releasebot.io/updates/openai/chatgpt)

OpenAI rolled out two healthcare plugins to ChatGPT Enterprise and EDU for eligible HIPAA-enabled workspaces: Healthcare Public Data (searching nine public medical sources including clinical trials, Medicare data, and provider records) and a second compliance-focused plugin. The expansion deepens OpenAI's push into regulated industries following its early healthcare partnership announcements.

### 10. Shield AI Raises $1.5B Series G at $12.7B Valuation
**Source:** [AI Funding Tracker](https://aifundingtracker.com/ai-startup-funding-news-today/) · [Crescendo AI](https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-vc-investment-deals-in-ai-startups)

Shield AI closed a $1.5 billion Series G (part of a $2.25B capital package), valuing the autonomous aviation startup at $12.7 billion — a 140% jump in one year. Shield AI builds the Hivemind autonomous pilot platform, which was selected by the U.S. Air Force for its Collaborative Combat Aircraft program, underscoring continued defense investment in AI-piloted systems.
