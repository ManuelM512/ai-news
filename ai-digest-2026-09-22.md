## New Model

### xAI Launches Grok 4.7 — Bigger Model, Same Price
**Source:** [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/378824/xai-launches-grok-4-7) · [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/21/spacex-launches-grok-4-7-with-long-horizon-processing-safety-upgrades/) · [llm-stats.com](https://llm-stats.com/blog/research/grok-4-7-launch)

xAI released Grok 4.7 on September 21 — its largest model yet at 2.1 trillion parameters (up 40% from Grok 4.6), trained with a longer RL run, improved self-verification, and supplemental data from SpaceX's Starlink telemetry and engineering failure logs. Pricing holds at $2/$6 per million input/output tokens, and the model ships immediately on the Grok app, Cursor, Grok Build, and the xAI API.

**Why it matters:** Grok 4.7's focus on long-horizon reasoning and hardware/physical-systems understanding — aided by real-world SpaceX data — gives it a potential edge for agentic and engineering workloads at unchanged cost.

## New Agent Tool

### Meta Muse Lands on macOS with Native App Access
**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/18/metas-muse-hits-mac-letting-the-ai-take-actions-on-your-computer/) · [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/meta-launches-muse-mac-app-with-file-messages-and-calendar-access/)

Meta shipped a standalone Muse app for macOS on September 17, giving its AI agent opt-in access to files, messages, calendar, notes, and mail directly inside native applications. Actions run through Meta's cloud Secure VM. Muse had already reached No. 1 on the US App Store within eight days of its September 8 iOS/Android debut, displacing ChatGPT.

**Why it matters:** Muse's Mac expansion is the most direct challenge yet to Apple Intelligence and OpenAI's desktop agents, bringing agentic computer-use into the hands of a consumer-scale audience with a product that is already a chart-topper.

## New Framework

### Google ADK for Kotlin 1.0 — Production-Ready Agents on Android and JVM
**Source:** [Google Developers Blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/announcing-adk-for-kotlin-10-building-production-ready-ai-agents-in-kotlin-android-and-beyond/) · [InfoQ](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/09/google-adk-1-0-released/)

Google's Agent Development Kit (ADK) for Kotlin reached 1.0 GA on September 9, achieving feature parity with the Python ADK while adding on-device and hybrid AI support for Android. Built on Kotlin Multiplatform, the framework includes hierarchical multi-agent orchestration, context compaction, session management, and KSP-powered compile-time tool schema generation (eliminating runtime reflection).

**Why it matters:** This brings the full ADK story to Android's 3-billion-device ecosystem and gives Kotlin/JVM shops a first-class path to building production agents without Python, lowering the barrier for enterprise mobile-AI integration.

## Research

### Devin Builds GPU Crypto Cracker, Factors RSA-260
**Source:** [Cognition Blog](https://cognition.com/blog/factoring-rsa-260) · [PostQuantum.com](https://postquantum.com/security-pqc/sa-260-factored-gpu-devin/)

Cognition engineer Eric Lu used the Devin AI agent to develop a GPU implementation of CADO-NFS (the general number field sieve), then ran it to factor RSA-260 — a 260-digit, 862-bit number and the largest RSA challenge number ever publicly factored. Devin produced a working GPU siever in ~9 hours given a single test GPU; the full computation required ~4,900 GPU-days.

**Why it matters:** This is a landmark demonstration of an AI coding agent tackling a long-horizon, highly technical research task largely autonomously. While RSA-2048 key sizes remain secure, the result accelerates the cryptographic community's timeline pressure on smaller legacy key deployments.

### OpenAI Proposes Global AI Alignment and RSI Standards
**Source:** [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/21/open-ai-alignment-rsi.html)

OpenAI released a framework calling for international standards around alignment research and recursive self-improvement (RSI), arguing that "alignment research must keep pace with capabilities so that systems built remain aligned with human values and under human control." The proposal calls for coordinated safety benchmarks across frontier labs and government bodies.

**Why it matters:** With multiple independent safety organizations warning that current alignment methods won't scale to the capability levels now in development, a coordinated standards push — if adopted — could set the governance floor for the next generation of models.

### Cohere and Aleph Alpha Sign Definitive Combination Agreement
**Source:** [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/16/cohere-and-aleph-alpha-agree-to-merge-in-reported-20b-deal/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/24/cohere-aleph-alpha-germany-ai-europe-expansion.html) · [BetaKit](https://betakit.com/cohere-reaches-terms-with-german-peer-aleph-alpha-to-take-on-ai-giants/)

Cohere and Aleph Alpha signed a definitive business combination agreement on September 16, formalizing the deal first announced in April 2026. The combined company will be dual-headquartered in Berlin and Toronto, operate globally as Cohere, and is valued at a reported ~$20 billion. Schwarz Gruppe (Lidl/Kaufland parent) anchored the deal with $600 million in investment. The merger is awaiting regulatory approval and is expected to close later this year.

**Why it matters:** The deal creates the first transatlantic "sovereign AI" champion — designed to serve EU data-residency and governance requirements — and directly challenges US hyperscalers in regulated sectors like public administration, defence, and healthcare.

### AI Takes 86% of All Global VC in H1 2026
**Source:** [SiliconANGLE / PitchBook](https://siliconangle.com/2026/07/09/pitchbook-us-venture-funding-hits-412-7b-first-half-ai-deals-dominate/)

PitchBook data shows AI companies captured $355.9 billion of the $412.7 billion in total US venture capital deployed in the first half of 2026 — roughly 86 cents of every venture dollar. The market is increasingly bifurcated: large checks continue flowing to frontier AI labs, while early-stage winners must show proof of revenue, defensible data, or domain-specific IP in verticals like legal, healthcare, robotics, and chips.

**Why it matters:** The funding concentration signals that the AI infrastructure layer is consolidating fast; startups unable to demonstrate enterprise traction or unique data moats face an increasingly difficult fundraising environment regardless of technical merit.
