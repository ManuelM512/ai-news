## Research

### 6. Falsification-Gated Self-Evolution for LLM Agents
**Source:** [ArXiv AI Digest — 2026-11-11/12](https://github.com/datnguyenquy94/news-radar/issues/1043)

New paper proposes that agents should save only lessons from prior tasks that have been explicitly validated for reuse and safety before updating their own behavior. This "falsification gate" prevents agents from propagating bad generalizations from isolated experiences.

**Why it matters:** Uncontrolled self-improvement in agents is a core alignment risk; principled gating mechanisms like this are crucial for safe long-running autonomous systems.

---

### 7. ReTrace: Distilling Tool-Using Agents into Planner-Free Workflows
**Source:** [ArXiv via news-radar digest](https://github.com/datnguyenquy94/news-radar/issues/1043)

ReTrace converts successful SOP-following agent trajectories into deterministic workflows that skip per-step LLM calls, dramatically reducing latency and cost while preserving correctness on structured tasks.

**Why it matters:** Distilling expensive agent runs into cheaper deterministic workflows is a practical path to making production agents economically viable at scale.

---

### 8. AutoScaffold: AI-Guided Scaffolding for Reinforcement Learning of LLMs
**Source:** [ArXiv via news-radar digest](https://github.com/datnguyenquy94/news-radar/issues/1043)

AutoScaffold uses AI-generated intermediate hints to construct richer reward signals for RL training, addressing sparse-reward problems in complex reasoning tasks without manual curriculum design.

**Why it matters:** Better reward shaping techniques directly accelerate the development of more capable reasoning models with less human engineering overhead.

## Other

### 9. Paris Call for AI Safety: Emergency Off Switches Committed
**Source:** [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)

France unveiled the Paris Call for AI Safety and Security, with OpenAI, Anthropic, and Mistral committing to build mandatory emergency shut-off mechanisms into their most advanced models. The agreement is non-binding but represents the first trilateral industry commitment on model kill-switches.

**Why it matters:** Coordinated industry commitment to emergency controls is a significant, if symbolic, governance milestone—especially with frontier labs as signatories alongside a G7 government.
