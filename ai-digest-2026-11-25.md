## New Model

### 1. OpenAI GPT-6.2 Astra & Sol
**Source:** [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-sol/) · [GCN](https://gcn.com/?p=23366) · [Digital Trends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/computing/openai-gpt-6-2-astra-sol-launch/)

OpenAI released two models on November 17: GPT-6.2 Astra (frontier, $8/M input, $40/M output) and GPT-6.2 Sol (efficient, $2/M input, $10/M output), available via ChatGPT, Codex, and the API. Notably the first model claimed to score 100 on ARC-AGI-3, though early user reception was mixed — some developers reported having Claude redo Astra's subagent outputs. The release follows OpenAI scrapping GPT-6.1 for safety reasons.

**Why it matters:** Sets a new public benchmark ceiling on ARC-AGI-3 and continues the competitive pressure on frontier model pricing.

## New Agent Tool

### 4. Microsoft Foundry: Agents as M365 Members
**Source:** [AI Agents Directory](https://aiagentsdirectory.com/news/ai-agents-news-brief-november-23-2026)

Microsoft detailed Foundry updates on November 19 that allow organizations to set up AI agents as named Microsoft 365 members with their own Entra identities, email inboxes, and calendar access. Agents can receive meeting invites, send emails, and participate in M365 workflows as first-class principals.

**Why it matters:** Bridges agent identity and enterprise IAM — a key step toward deploying agents inside corporate collaboration environments with auditable, permissioned identities.

---

### 5. Tether 0.4.0 — Agent Run Replay & Testing
**Source:** [AI Agent Store News](https://aiagentstore.ai/ai-agent-news/this-week)

Tether released version 0.4.0 on November 22, adding a test harness that records and replays agent runs. Developers can verify which tools were called, in what order, and whether the agent stayed within configured step or cost limits. Replays are deterministic against the recorded trace.

**Why it matters:** Fills a major gap in agent observability — reproducible regression testing for multi-step agent behaviors without re-spending compute or hitting live APIs.

## Research

### 7. Anthropic: AI-Authored Complexity Theory Proof (VP ≠ VNP)
**Source:** [AI Daily Briefing](https://sixsixai.com/news/2026-11-24-ai-news) · [LLM News](https://llm-stats.com/ai-news)

Anthropic published a 70-page paper claiming to resolve Valiant's VP ≠ VNP conjecture over every field, credited to an internal Claude research model. The proof includes a Lean 4 formalization that passed automated verification, but as of publication date no human mathematician has independently reviewed the full argument.

**Why it matters:** If the proof holds up to human expert scrutiny, it would be one of the first AI-authored results to resolve a major open problem in computational complexity — with significant implications for cryptography and algorithm theory.

---

### 8. Google DeepMind: Gemini 4 Agent Teams Advance Quantum Algorithms
**Source:** [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)

Google DeepMind reported that multi-agent teams of Gemini 4 models generated and improved quantum circuits for fusion energy modelling, chemistry, and enzyme simulation. All results were formally verified in Lean.

**Why it matters:** Demonstrates a practical workflow where LLM agents can operate in specialized scientific domains and produce formally verifiable outputs — a step toward AI as a genuine co-researcher in quantum computing.

### 10. Amazon in Talks to Acquire Decart AI (~$7.5B)
**Source:** [India Tech Network](https://indiatechnetwork.substack.com/p/amazon-makes-a-strategic-ai-infrastructure)

Amazon is reportedly in talks to pay approximately $7.5 billion for most of Decart AI, whose optimization software can run generative AI workloads on AWS Trainium chips at roughly half the cost of GPU-based inference. The deal would bring Decart's team and the Decart Optimization Stack in-house.

**Why it matters:** A major infrastructure bet: owning the software stack that maximises Trainium efficiency would give Amazon a durable cost advantage on its own AI cloud over GPU-dependent competitors.
