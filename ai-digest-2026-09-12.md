## New Model

### 1. Sakana AI Releases Fugu Max and Fugu Ultra v2
**Source:** [Sakana AI Blog](https://sakana.ai/fugu-max-release/) · [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/09/10/sakana-ai-launches-fugu-max-and-fugu-ultra-v2-for-cheaper-stronger-multi-agent-orchestration/)

Sakana AI shipped **Fugu Max** and **Fugu Ultra v2** on September 11, 2026. Fugu is an orchestrator model—rather than a single model, it routes each sub-task to the best model in its pool and stitches the answers back together into one response. Fugu Ultra v2 targets complex reasoning, autonomous research, and full-stack software engineering; it offers a 1M-token context window, 128K max output, and is priced at $5.00/$30.00 per 1M input/output tokens.

**Why it matters:** Fugu Ultra v2 is best or joint-best on five of eight benchmarks in Sakana's evaluation—including 48.3 on Chartography (vs. Claude Opus 5 at 27.3) and 74.3 on DeepSWE. The orchestration-as-a-model approach lets developers call one API endpoint and get best-of-pool quality automatically, a notable step toward practical multi-model agent routing.

### 2. DeepSeek Releases V4.1 Flash: Multimodal MoE with 1M Context and Open Weights
**Source:** [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/09/10/deepseek-ai-released-deepseek-v4-1-flash-with-1m-context-fp4-kv-cache-and-cross-layer-attention-reuse/) · [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2026/09/11/deepseek-v4-1-flash-launches-with-lower-prices-and-native-vision/)

DeepSeek released **DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash** on September 10–11, replacing V4 Flash and V4 Flash Vision Experimental. The model uses a new Causal Encoder-Decoder architecture with ~748B total parameters (only 8B active during prefill, 16B during decode), a 1M-token context window, native multimodal vision (up to ~1344×1344px), and FP4 KV cache that is ~4× smaller than its predecessor. It was trained on 45 trillion tokens and open-weighted under MIT on Hugging Face (`deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash`).

**Why it matters:** V4.1 Flash delivers competitive performance while cutting inference costs, and the MIT open-weights release means the community can self-host a capable multimodal MoE without licensing restrictions—meaningful pressure on closed-source provider pricing.

## New Framework

### 3. Microsoft Agent Framework 1.18.0: Unified Vector Store Abstractions
**Source:** [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/microsoft/agent-framework/releases) · [Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/microsoft)

Microsoft shipped **Agent Framework 1.18.0** with a comprehensive vector-store layer: shared abstractions with portable filters, an in-memory vector store, Azure AI Search integration, Redis HASH/JSON vector stores, and alpha connectors for Qdrant and PostgreSQL/pgvector. The release also adds stronger AG-UI and core workflow handling plus MCP history support.

**Why it matters:** Having a single, swappable vector-store interface baked into the agent framework removes a major integration pain point—teams can switch between cloud-managed (Azure AI Search) and self-hosted (Qdrant, pgvector) backends without rewriting retrieval logic.

**Why it matters:** As enterprise AI agent adoption accelerates—forecasts put task-specific agents in ~40% of enterprise apps by end of 2026—agent UX is becoming a first-class engineering concern.
