## Research · Shadow Memory for Long-Horizon LLM Agent Safety (arXiv)

**Source:** [DailyArXiv — September 25, 2026](https://github.com/xrose3159/DailyArXiv/issues/223)

A paper accepted at ACM CCS 2026 proposes **Shadow Memory**, a technique for safeguarding LLM agents against long-horizon threats. The approach maintains a hidden, agent-inaccessible memory layer that tracks cumulative intent and detects when an agent's behavior drifts toward unsafe goals over multi-step tasks.

**Why it matters:** As agents take on longer-horizon tasks, standard per-turn safety checks fail; Shadow Memory addresses a fundamental gap in agentic safety architecture that will become increasingly critical as autonomous agents operate across longer time horizons.
