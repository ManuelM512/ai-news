### Google A2A Joins AAIF — 250+ Members Under Linux Foundation Governance
**Source:** [AI Agent Store — Week of August 24](https://aiagentstore.ai/ai-agent-news/this-week) · [Agentic AI News](https://agentic.ai/news)

On August 20, Google's Agent-to-Agent (A2A) protocol was formally admitted to the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) under the Linux Foundation, joining Anthropic's MCP under neutral governance. AAIF now counts over 250 members including AWS, Anthropic, Block, Bloomberg, Cloudflare, Google, Microsoft, and OpenAI. **Why it matters:** Bringing the two dominant agent communication protocols under a single neutral body sharply reduces the risk of fragmentation and should accelerate interoperable multi-agent ecosystems.

## Research

### DeepMind: Training Methodology Reduces "Reasoning Collapse" by ~40%
**Source:** [Skycrumbs — AI Research August 2026](https://skycrumbs.com/blog/ai-research-august-2026)

A DeepMind team published a paper targeting what they call "reasoning collapse" — the failure mode where a model solves a problem correctly in one framing but fails on an equivalent problem stated differently. Their proposed training methodology reduces this brittleness by approximately 40% on their benchmark. **Why it matters:** Robustness to problem reformulation is a prerequisite for deploying reasoning models in high-stakes settings; a 40% reduction in brittleness is a meaningful step toward reliable agentic reasoning.

---

### DeepMind Vision-Language Paper: Reducing Hallucination in Multimodal Models
**Source:** [Skycrumbs — AI Research August 2026](https://skycrumbs.com/blog/ai-research-august-2026)

A separate DeepMind team published a method for significantly reducing factual errors in vision-language models, addressing one of the most persistent reliability issues in multimodal AI. The August research theme broadly shifted from raw capability to reliability, safety evaluation maturity, and practical scientific application. **Why it matters:** Hallucination in vision-language models remains a key blocker for medical imaging, autonomous inspection, and document understanding; even modest reductions unlock new deployment categories.
