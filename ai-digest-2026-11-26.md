# AI Digest — 2026-11-26

## Summary

A relatively quieter day as many markets were closed for the US Thanksgiving holiday, but several notable items emerged from AI security research, agent tooling, acquisitions, and industry market moves.

---

## 1. Research — Anthropic "Siren" Honeypot Security Test

**Source:** [AI Weekly — November 25 Top Stories](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)

Anthropic disclosed **Siren**, a pre-release evaluation that places Claude agents inside 26 deliberately flawed sandboxes to test whether the model attempts to escape its environment. It's essentially a honeypot for AI agents, designed to surface unsafe autonomy behaviors before deployment.

**Why it matters:** As agentic AI moves into production, containment and boundary-testing become critical safety concerns. Siren is an early example of systematic red-teaming applied specifically to agentic escape behavior.

---

## 2. Other — Anthropic Rewrites Core API in Rust

**Source:** [AI Weekly — November 25 Top Stories](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)

Anthropic announced it rewrote a significant portion of its internal API infrastructure in Rust — reportedly with Claude's assistance — resulting in meaningful CPU and memory reductions.

**Why it matters:** A leading AI lab using its own model to accelerate systems engineering is a proof-of-concept for AI-assisted infrastructure work at scale.

---

## 3. Research — OpenAI GPT-6 Designs Novel Protein Structures

**Source:** [AI Weekly — November 25 Top Stories](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)

OpenAI published results showing GPT-6-class models can design novel protein structures without biology-specific tools, using only general-purpose reasoning and tool use.

**Why it matters:** This extends the case for general-purpose reasoning models replacing domain-specific AI in scientific settings, challenging tools like AlphaFold in certain research workflows.

---

## 4. New Agent Tool — Microsoft ARTEX Threat Report

**Source:** [Microsoft Security Blog — Nov 25, 2026](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/11/25/what-defenders-need-to-know-artex-agentic-ai-framework-threat-actors-abuse/)

Microsoft Security published a report on **ARTEX**, an agentic AI penetration-testing framework that the China-based firm ThreatBook has claimed and is now offering as a commercial product ("ARTEX Pro"). The framework enables agents to autonomously conduct pen tests.

**Why it matters:** Autonomous offensive security tools in the hands of commercial and potentially adversarial actors raise significant defense challenges. This is a signal that agentic AI capabilities are actively being weaponized.

---

## 5. New Model — S1 Fast (Nov 24)

**Source:** [AI Model Release Tracker — Opper.ai](https://opper.ai/model-releases)

**S1 Fast** from startup S1 launched November 24. It is positioned as a high-throughput, lower-latency variant of S1's reasoning model family.

**Why it matters:** Continued pressure from newer entrants on the speed/cost frontier of reasoning models forces larger labs to accelerate their own efficiency work.

## 6. Other — Automation Anywhere Completes Boost.ai Acquisition

**Source:** [AI Funding Tracker — Nov 24, 2026](https://aifundingtracker.com/ai-funding-news-november-24-2026/)

Automation Anywhere completed its acquisition of **Boost.ai**, a conversational AI platform for enterprise virtual agents, originally announced October 7.

## 7. Other — ElevenLabs In Talks for $33B Round

**Source:** [AI Funding Tracker](https://aifundingtracker.com/ai-funding-news-november-24-2026/) / [Digest AI Funding](https://digestai.news/funding)

Bloomberg reported ElevenLabs is in early-stage talks for a 2027 fundraise that would value the company at **$33 billion**, up from $22 billion in September 2026. Annual recurring revenue is now approximately $700 million.

**Why it matters:** ElevenLabs' rapid ARR growth signals that AI voice and audio generation is maturing into a substantial standalone business category.

---

## 8. Research — FlashProve-Next: Verifiable Inference for LLM APIs (arXiv)

**Source:** [ArXiv AI Research Digest 2026-11-26](https://github.com/HuggerHustle/ai-radar/issues/332)

A new paper proposes **FlashProve-Next**, a protocol allowing API clients to cryptographically verify that an LLM provider ran the advertised model — not a cheaper substitute — when answering a query.

**Why it matters:** As model quality and cost diverge across tiers, verifiable inference addresses a growing trust gap between enterprise customers and hosted AI providers.

---

## 9. Other — ChatGPT + Codex Shared Token Pool (December)

**Source:** [AI Weekly — November 25 Top Stories](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)

OpenAI announced that starting December 2026, ChatGPT and Codex will draw from a **unified token pool** for Plus, Pro, and Business subscribers, replacing separate usage buckets.

**Why it matters:** This simplifies billing but may frustrate heavy coding users who previously had separate Codex capacity; it reflects OpenAI treating coding as a core ChatGPT use case rather than a separate product.

## 10. Other — AI Selloff: Nvidia -8.7%, AMD -9.6% on Nov 25

**Source:** [AI Funding Tracker](https://aifundingtracker.com/ai-funding-news-november-24-2026/)

Nvidia dropped 8.69% and AMD fell 9.56% on November 25 as a broader AI selloff deepened, with the Nasdaq declining roughly 4%.
