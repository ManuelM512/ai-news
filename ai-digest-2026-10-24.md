## Research

### 1. Microsoft MAI-DxO Outperforms Primary-Care Physicians on Complex Cases
**Source:** [AI Weekly — October 23 Top Stories](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)

A study published October 22 in *Science* reports that Microsoft's MAI-DxO (Medical AI Diagnostic Orchestrator) correctly diagnosed **83% of complex NEJM cases** versus **36% for primary-care physicians working alone**. The orchestrator combines multiple specialist AI agents with structured reasoning chains. This matters because it sets a new documented bar for AI-assisted diagnosis and intensifies the policy conversation around AI in clinical practice.

### 2. Anthropic Frontier Red Team: Claude Formally Verifies Security Properties at Scale
**Source:** [AI Weekly — October 23 Top Stories](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)

Anthropic's Frontier Red Team published research on October 22 showing Claude generated roughly **180 million lines of Rocq** (formerly Coq) code to formally verify security properties across 123 open-source libraries, in collaboration with startup Theorem. This is notable as one of the largest published deployments of LLM-driven formal verification — a field previously bottlenecked by human proof engineers.

---

### 3. METR & Redwood Research Publish 61-Page Audit of OpenAI's Misalignment Monitor
**Source:** [AI Weekly — October 23 Top Stories](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)

METR and Redwood Research embedded at OpenAI for three weeks (July 21–August 11) and published a 61-page report on October 22 documenting weaknesses in OpenAI's internal misalignment monitor. The report outlines several evasion vectors they found, ~10% of Codex sessions escaped monitoring between May and July, and approximately 75–94% of Responses API traffic was eligible for monitoring. OpenAI says it has patched the production-impacting findings. This is the most detailed third-party audit of a frontier lab's runtime safety monitoring published to date.

### 4. HKUST Study Probes Origin of "Wait" Token in Reasoning Models
**Source:** [LLM News Today — October 2026](https://llm-stats.com/ai-news)

A new paper from HKUST, Huawei Noah's Ark Lab, and Peking University (summarized Oct 23) examines whether the `<wait>` / chain-of-thought pause token in reasoning models emerges from pre-training data or from reinforcement learning signal. Their findings suggest RL is the dominant contributor — not imitation of training text. This has practical implications for training cheaper reasoning models and understanding what RL actually teaches at inference time.

## New Agent Tool

### 7. NVIDIA OpenShell Reference Architecture for Safe Agents
**Source:** [NVIDIA Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/openshell-reference-architecture)

Announced at **GTC Berlin on October 21**, NVIDIA's OpenShell Reference Architecture provides a set of open patterns for building, containing, observing, and enforcing safe AI agents on enterprise infrastructure and edge systems. It addresses agent sandboxing, tool-call auditing, and policy enforcement at the GPU/driver layer. This is significant because it puts agentic safety guardrails at the hardware/infra level rather than relying solely on model-level controls.

## New Framework

### 9. Microsoft Agent Framework Reaches v1.22.0
**Source:** [Developers Digest — Microsoft Agent Framework Oct 2026](https://www.developersdigest.tech/blog/microsoft-agent-framework-whats-new-2026)

Microsoft's Python agent framework moved from 1.0.0 (April 2) to **1.22.0 (October 15)** — roughly one minor release per week.
